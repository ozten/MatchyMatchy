FAQs

What is Hiya Branded Call, and how is it different from regular caller ID or Number Registration?

Hiya Branded Call lets you display your business name, and in many cases, your logo and call reason on a customer’s phone screen — when their device and network support branded displays. This helps recipients instantly recognize your business and builds trust before they answer.

Here’s how it differs from other caller ID solutions:

  • Caller ID (CLI): Shows only the phone number of the caller.
  • CNAM: Adds a business name (up to 15 characters) but works only on landlines in the U.S. and Canada and relies on outdated databases.
  • Branded Caller ID: Provides a modern, customizable caller ID experience for mobile phones, showing your business identity on supported networks and devices.
  • Number Registration: Confirms your business as a trusted caller and reduces spam labeling risk but does not change what recipients see.

Learn more about our free Number Registration service, included with all Branded Call plans.

Will Branded Call prevent my calls from being marked as spam?

Branded Call reduces the chance of your calls being flagged as spam by showing recipients a trusted, branded display instead of an unknown number. However, it doesn’t guarantee 100% protection. Carriers also consider consumer feedback and compliance with government regulations, so maintaining customer-friendly calling practices is key.

To ensure your numbers are verified, start with free Number Registration.

Which carriers and countries support Hiya Branded Call?

Branded Call coverage varies by region and device type:

  • U.S.: Verizon, T-Mobile, and unlocked Samsung phones (not currently on AT&T).
  • Canada: Rogers, Bell, and unlocked Samsung phones.
  • United Kingdom: EE, BT, VMO2 (Virgin Media O2) and unlocked Samsung phones.
  • Australia: Unlocked Samsung phones only.

Branded Call also works globally on unlocked Samsung devices, further extending your reach. See plan details on our Connect Pricing Page.

How long does it take for my branding to appear after purchase?

Once you’ve uploaded your numbers and set up your branding in the Hiya platform, your branded display typically goes live within a few hours to a couple of days. This time allows Hiya to verify your business details and ensure your numbers are properly configured across the carrier network.

Does Hiya Branded Call integrate with my dialer or calling software?

No integration is required. Branded Call works at the network level, so you simply upload your phone numbers into the Hiya platform and set your branding. It’s fast, seamless, and code-free.

How does Hiya protect against spoofing and fraudulent calls?

Hiya prevents spoofed calls from displaying your brand by authenticating every outbound call at the network level. When your system places a call, it first sends a brief API “pre-announcement” that allows Hiya to verify the call’s origin and match it to your registered business numbers.

Only authenticated calls are eligible to display your branded caller ID on participating networks. Spoofed or unverified calls are either rejected or shown without branding, helping protect your customers from fraud.

This network-level verification ensures that when people see your branded caller ID, they can trust it’s really you.
